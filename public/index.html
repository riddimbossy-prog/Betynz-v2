HOW THE SHORTLIST WORKS

Evidence first.
Risk stays visible.

  1. Compare the available markets

    Every returned Sportybet market is retained. Eligible outcomes must be active and priced from 1.20 to 1.50, including both limits.

  2. Read the matchup

    Home and away form, league positions, H2H, score transitions and available advanced statistics inform the estimate.

  3. Choose one final market

    The highest estimated chance wins the comparison. Value, uncertainty and failure scenarios remain visible alongside it.
