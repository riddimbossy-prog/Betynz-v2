# Chronos HT/FT Momentum Engine

Chronos reuses the cached five-home/five-away history already collected by Betynz and begins with first-half/full-time transitions.

Transitions: WW, DW, LW, WD, DD, LD, WL, DL, LL.

Routes include lead-and-hold, level-at-half acceleration, comeback/swing goals and early-event goals. Current form, venue momentum and offered HT/FT or safer fallback markets must support the transition evidence. Five venue matches and at least three usable half-time samples per team are required.
