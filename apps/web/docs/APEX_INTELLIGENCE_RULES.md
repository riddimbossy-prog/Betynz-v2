# Apex Intelligence Engine rules

Apex Intelligence complements the independent PPG Route. Venue PPG is one input inside Apex, but it cannot independently produce an Apex selection.

## Evidence blocks

Apex scores six independent evidence families:

1. Relative venue strength and five-match points.
2. Recent-three acceleration.
3. Attack-versus-defence fit.
4. Defensive control or vulnerability.
5. Goal-behaviour consistency.
6. Exact market and price confirmation.

## Direct pick gate

A direct pick requires all of the following:

- Five verified home matches and five verified away matches.
- At least four independent non-market evidence families.
- Composite score of 82 or higher.
- Exact selected market available from API-Football.
- No named hard blocker.
- No near-equal opposite qualified route.

## Safer pick gate

A safer pick requires:

- At least three independent non-market evidence families.
- Composite score of 72 or higher.
- Approved safer market available.
- No named hard blocker.

## Candidate routes

- Home composite superiority.
- Away composite superiority.
- Composite attacking game.
- Composite controlled game.
- Two-way scoring profile.
- One-side scoring suppression.

Only one official selection is published per fixture. Near-equal opposite directions produce a conflict instead of a pick.
