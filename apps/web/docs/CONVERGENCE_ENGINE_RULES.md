# Convergence Engine

The Convergence Engine is independent from Market Route and PPG Route.

It scores four evidence blocks, each worth 25 points:

1. Attack
2. Defence
3. Venue
4. Market

A candidate needs support from at least three blocks.

- 78–100 with the exact target market available: FIRE
- 70–77 with an approved safer market available: SAFER
- Below 70, fewer than three supporting blocks, a hard blocker, or an unavailable market: NO PICK

Direct Over/Under conflicts within five score points are rejected.

Supported first-release routes:

- Over 1.5
- Over 2.5, safer Over 1.5
- Under 3.5
- Under 2.5, safer Under 3.5
- BTTS Yes, safer Over 1.5
- BTTS No, safer Under 3.5
- Stronger Team Win, safer Double Chance

The score is an evidence-agreement score, not a guaranteed probability.
